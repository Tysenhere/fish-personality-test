What’s Your Fish! Personality?

Follow Bubbles the Blue Tang on a very eventful first day at work. Choose what you would most likely do, and discover which Fish! Principle reflects you best.

🐢 Be There — Sea Turtle 🐬 Make Their Day — Dolphin 🦈 Choose Your Attitude — Shark 🐠 Play — Clownfish
Story time: Bubbles the Blue Tang has just joined Deep Central Sea Ltd (DCS). It is only 8.31am and Bubbles has already swum into the wrong meeting room, waved at a poster thinking it was a colleague, and accidentally replied “Thanks, you too!” when the coffee machine beeped. A true first-day icon.

Help Bubbles survive the day by choosing what you would do in each situation. There are no wrong answers — only different types of workplace sea-creature energy.

Question 1

Bubbles reaches the reception reef and sees another new colleague looking more lost than a prawn at a karaoke contest. What do you do?

Question 2

During orientation, Bubbles’ name tag says “Bubblesss” with three S. The trainer asks if everything is correct. What do you do?

Question 3

Bubbles is given a long staff handbook that looks thicker than a whale’s diary. What is your first move?

Question 4

At lunch, Bubbles sits with a group of colleagues but the table goes quiet. Even the seaweed is uncomfortable. What do you do?

Question 5

Bubbles accidentally enters a meeting 10 minutes early and everyone looks very serious. What do you do?

Question 6

A colleague seems stressed because their printer refuses to cooperate. It is making noises like an angry crab. What do you do?

Question 7

Bubbles receives feedback on the first task of the day. Some parts went well, some parts need improvement. What feels most like you?

Question 8

The team needs a volunteer for a small activity at the next staff gathering. Bubbles looks around and sees many sudden “busy with email” faces. What do you do?

Question 9

Near the end of the day, Bubbles realises they forgot one colleague’s name. Panic bubbles appear. What do you do?

Question 10

After helping Bubbles survive the first day at Deep Central Sea Ltd (DCS), Bubbles turns to you and asks, “So… how are YOU feeling after all this?” How do you reply?

Oops! Please answer all questions before revealing your result. Even Bubbles needs full data before swimming dramatically away.

Manual backup: If the result button does not work on your intranet, count your answers manually. Highest score wins: Sea Turtle = Be There, Dolphin = Make Their Day, Shark = Choose Your Attitude, Clownfish = Play.
